# flopy confined aquifer analysis
FloPy-based groundwater flow simulations in a heterogeneous confined aquifer, including steady-state and transient analyses, risk assessment, and parameter sensitivity evaluation.
The objective of this study was to perform a steady-state simulation of flow in a hetero
geneous confined aquifer influenced by multiple extraction wells. The study analyzes the
hydraulic head distribution and evaluates the discrepancies between numerical outcomes
(MODFLOW) and analytical solutions (Thiem equations).
